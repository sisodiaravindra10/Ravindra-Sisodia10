About
Product Designer with 5 years of experience bridging the gap between high-fidelity design and production code. Expert in Figma-native design systems, variables, and tokens — now enhanced by AI-augmented workflows using Claude Code, Figma MCP, and Tailwind to move from product briefs to functional mocks in a single session.
Starting as a Junior Designer at FirstCry.com, I grew into a Design Lead managing a team of 6 at ArtWorksIT, shipping 30+ websites for enterprise clients. After honing my craft in finance-sector apps at ScreenRoot — designing for Yes Bank, Bandhan Bank, and ICICI Bank — I joined Zop.Dev where I now pioneer AI-powered design-to-code workflows that cut iteration time from days to minutes.
Ravindra Sisodia, a product designer based in Bengaluru, specialises in design systems, AI-augmented workflows, and bridging the gap between design and engineering.
My design philosophy: every design decision must earn its place in code. I hold a B.VOC in Digital Arts & Animation from Fergusson College, Pune — and have spent the last 5 years turning that foundation into a practice that lives at the intersection of craft, systems thinking, and engineering collaboration.

Design Process

An AI-augmented design methodology — from brief to production-ready in a single session.

01
Discover
Stakeholder interviews, user research, and competitive analysis — scoped fast using Notion and Figma FigJam to align teams before a single frame is drawn.
02
Define
Problem statements, user flows, and system requirements crystallised into a Figma-first design brief with variables, tokens, and component scope pre-mapped.
03
Design
High-fidelity Figma mocks (75–80% production fidelity) built with a structured design system — variables, auto-layout, and developer-ready components from the first frame.
04
AI-Augment
Claude Code + Figma MCP + Tailwind translate designs into functional, production-ready code — reducing handoff friction and cutting iteration time from days to minutes.
05
Ship
Markdown-documented design systems, annotated Figma files, and AI-assisted developer handoff ensure what ships matches what was designed — every time.
